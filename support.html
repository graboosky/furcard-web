Reminders

  • A reminder didn't come. Check that notifications are on for Furcard, and that a Focus isn't holding it back: turn on Time Sensitive notifications for Furcard. Settings › Reminders shows "Reminders set until…". Open Furcard now and then, so it can plan the next days.
  • Alarms. Turn on "Ring like an alarm" for a medicine and allow alarms when Furcard asks. An alarm rings even in silent mode. The iPhone limits how many alarms an app may set at once; past that limit, doses come as reminders.

The widget, the Lock Screen and Siri

  • The widget. Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Furcard. On the Lock Screen, customize it and add Furcard's widget.
  • Siri. Say "Log a dose in Furcard", "What's the next dose in Furcard", "Record for the vet in Furcard" or "Show a concern in Furcard".
  • Visit today. On a visit's day, opening Furcard shows the time to leave and the morning's prep on the Lock Screen. You can turn it off in Settings.

Backup, a new iPhone, and your data

  • A full backup. In Settings › Backup › Save a Full Backup…, choose a place other than this iPhone: Files, a computer or a drive. On a new iPhone, open the file, or use Restore…. Restore replaces everything with the backup, and first keeps a copy of today.
  • Daily copies. Furcard keeps a copy of your pets' details for a week, without photos or videos, in iCloud Drive › Furcard — or on the iPhone when iCloud Drive is off.
  • Deleting. - A pet: open its profile and choose Delete. - Everything: delete Furcard. - The copies: delete the Furcard folder in Files.

SOS

The poison lines on SOS are run by others, and some charge a fee. Furcard isn't affiliated with them. Found a wrong number? Write to us, and we will check it at once.

Privacy

Furcard keeps your pets' information on your iPhone. The developer never receives it. Read the privacy policy.
